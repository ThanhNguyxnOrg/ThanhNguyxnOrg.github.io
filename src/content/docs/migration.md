A meaningful part of ThanhNguyxn's workspace is dedicated to restoring and continuing work from earlier stages of my GitHub journey.

## Why migration matters here

Some projects deserve:

- a cleaner home
- better structure
- stronger documentation
- a second life
- a better future

## Migration can mean

Depending on the repository, migration may be:

- a direct re-upload
- a cleaned-up rewrite
- a modernized rebuild
- a continuation of older work
- an archived snapshot for history

## Why repo quality may vary

Because repositories come from different learning phases, the code style and structure may not always match perfectly. That is expected. This workspace reflects:

- where I started
- what I rebuilt
- what I'm becoming

## Goal

The goal is not only to preserve old code. The goal is to preserve growth and build forward from it.
